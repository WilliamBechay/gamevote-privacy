Ce qui ne quitte jamais ta machine

Tes identifiants de jeu

C’est le point le plus important de ce document, et c’est une décision d’architecture, pas une promesse.

Les mots de passe que tu saisis pour un portail de récompenses sont chiffrés par Windows (safeStorage, qui s’appuie sur DPAPI) et stockés dans un fichier local. Ils ne sont envoyés nulle part : le serveur n’a aucune colonne pour les recevoir, le format des adaptateurs ne peut pas les transporter, et aucun canal interne de l’application ne permet de relire une valeur — il est possible d’écrire un identifiant et de demander lesquels sont renseignés, jamais d’en obtenir un.

Si le système d’exploitation ne peut pas chiffrer, l’application refuse d’enregistrer et te le dit, plutôt que d’écrire un mot de passe en clair.

Tes réglages locaux

Position et taille du minuteur, mise en pause, lancement au démarrage : tout cela reste dans un fichier sur ta machine.

Supabase intervient comme sous-traitant et encadre ces transferts par les clauses contractuelles types de la Commission européenne. Tu as le droit de le savoir avant de créer un compte, et c’est pourquoi c’est écrit ici plutôt qu’en note de bas de page.

Privacy Policy

GameVote — also distributed as GameBots.

Last updated: 30 October 2026. The French version above is authoritative; this is a translation.

GameVote is a Windows app that keeps track of your daily Minecraft server votes, their order and their 24-hour cooldowns.

Who is responsible

William Bechay, an individual, in France.
Contact: snow231231@gmail.com

What never leaves your machine

Your game credentials. This is the most important line here, and it is an architectural decision rather than a promise. Passwords you enter for a reward portal are encrypted by Windows (safeStorage, which uses DPAPI) and kept in a local file. They are sent nowhere: the server has no column to receive them, the adapter format cannot carry them, and no internal channel can read one back — a credential can be written, and the app can be asked which keys are set, never for a value. If the operating system cannot encrypt, the app refuses to store and says so, rather than writing a plaintext password.

Your local settings. Timer position and size, pause state, launch at login: all of it stays in a file on your machine.

What is sent to the server

Your email address and hashed password; your display name; your in-game username (it is copied into the vote pages’ field); your vote history (which site, when); a device identifier with the device name, platform and app version, used to count the devices your licence allows; your licence tier and status; your purchases (product, amount, Stripe reference); the outcome of automatic claim runs (game, status, error code); and your draw entries.

If you sign in with Google, Google passes us your email address and name. Nothing else — the only scopes requested are email, profile and openid. Your Google password never passes through the app: sign-in opens in your real browser precisely so that the app cannot see it.

What is not collected

  • No telemetry, no usage analytics, no advertising trackers.
  • No card numbers. Payment happens on a page hosted by Stripe; the app only receives a transaction reference.
  • No game credentials, as above.

Where your data is stored

The database and authentication are hosted by Supabase in the us-east-1 region — in the United States. If you are in the European Union, your data is therefore transferred outside the EU. Supabase acts as a processor and covers those transfers with the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Third-party services

Supabase (database and accounts) sees everything listed above. Stripe sees your email and payment method. Google sees that you signed in to GameVote, if you choose that. GitHub sees your IP address when the app checks for an update. Microsoft handles distribution if you installed from the Store. The vote sites see your username and IP address when you vote.

You do the voting. The app opens the page in your browser and puts your username on the clipboard. It does not fill the form for you, does not submit anything, and does not get past any bot check. Your relationship with those sites is direct.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists. Vote history is kept because it is what the cooldowns are calculated from; it has no other use. Payment records are kept longer, for as long as accounting obligations require.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, export it, or delete all of it, your account included. Write to the contact address above; requests are answered within one month.

Deleting your account removes the rows that belong to you. Game credentials were never with us: they go when you uninstall the app and delete its data folder.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and no data is knowingly collected about them.
